Return & Refund Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

FH EMPIRE aims to provide customers with reliable digital services and fair resolution of order-related issues. Please review the following Return and Refund Policy before placing an order.

1. Digital Products & Services

Most FH EMPIRE products and services are digital in nature. Once a digital product, coins, top-up, or other digital service has been successfully delivered to the account or ID provided by the customer, it generally cannot be returned, reversed, or refunded.

2. Undelivered Orders

If payment has been successfully received but FH EMPIRE is unable to deliver the purchased product or service, the case will be reviewed and the customer may be offered a refund, replacement, adjustment, or another appropriate resolution.

3. Incorrect Information Provided by Customer

Customers are responsible for providing correct account IDs, usernames, contact details, and other information required to complete an order. If a service is successfully delivered according to the information provided by the customer, a refund may not be available for an incorrect ID or account submitted by the customer.

4. Duplicate or Extra Payments

If a customer accidentally makes a duplicate or excess payment, they should contact FH EMPIRE with the relevant Order ID and payment proof. After verification, the extra amount may be refunded or adjusted against another order, as appropriate.

5. Orders We Cannot Complete

If FH EMPIRE cannot complete an order after receiving payment, we will work with the customer to provide an appropriate resolution, which may include a refund or an alternative service with the customer's agreement.

6. Third-Party Platforms

FH EMPIRE may provide services related to third-party platforms. Once an ordered service has been successfully delivered, subsequent actions taken by a third-party platform, including account restrictions, suspensions, policy changes, or other platform-related actions, do not automatically qualify the order for a refund.

7. Physical Products

Where FH EMPIRE offers physical products, applicable return, replacement, delivery, and refund conditions may be provided with the relevant product or order. Such conditions may vary depending on the nature and condition of the product.

8. Requesting a Refund

To report a payment or order issue, customers should contact FH EMPIRE through our official support channel and provide their Order ID, contact number, payment proof, and relevant order details. Requests will be reviewed before a refund or other resolution is approved.

9. Refund Processing

Approved refunds will normally be processed through the original or another appropriate payment method. Processing time may vary depending on the bank, wallet, payment gateway, or payment service provider involved.

Need Help?

If you have an issue with an order or payment, please contact FH EMPIRE through our official customer support channel with your Order ID and payment details.
